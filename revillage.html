Resident Evil Village BioTracker

Total Achievements: 56

Story:

  • Survive the lycan attack once entering the village.
  • Escape the mines after encountering the lords.
  • Defeat Lady Dimitrescu’s mutated form.
  • Defeat Donna and Angie in House Beneviento.
  • Defeat Moreau’s mutated form.
  • Defeat Uriaș when you’re in the stronghold.
  • Place the Giant’s Chalice containing the four flasks in the ceremony site.
  • Defeat Heisenberg’s mutated form.
  • Find the underground ruins containing the Megamycete.

Challenges:

  • Defeat Vârcolac Alfa, you can find Vârcolac after defeating Moreau in the Fallow Plot of the village.
  • Defeat Uriaș during the first lycan attack, this can be done easily in NG+.
  • Complete the story using four or less recovery items. This can be done easily in NG+ on the casual difficulty.
  • Complete the story in 3 hours or less.
  • Complete the story without spending over 10,000 lei.
  • Complete the game using knives only. If the game forces you to use another weapon, it is excluded. This can be done easiest with the LZ Answerer, a weapon unlockable by achieving an SS rank on all stages in The Mercenaries.

Collectables:

  • Destroy 1 of 20 Goats of Warding. Refer to "Heretic" for more information.
  • Solve a Labyrinth, there are four total with one for each Lord.
  • Combine treasure into its complete form. Examine the treasure to determine if it’s incomplete.
  • Complete the village map by walking to all accessible places.
  • Open the door to all 10 outhouses in the village in one playthrough.
  • View all character and weapon models from the extra content shop.
  • Break all 19 breakable windows in Castle Dimitrescu in one playthrough.
  • View all the concept art from the extra content shop.
  • Read all 47 files in the story.
  • Equip all the guns with their customizable parts.
  • Collect all of the Duke’s crafting recipes.
  • Destroy all 20 Goats of Warding.
  • Craft every item in the crafting menu.

Miscellaneous:

  • Craft an item.
  • Make a purchase from the Duke.
  • Unlock a lock with a lockpick.
  • Equip a gun with its customizable part.
  • Hunt an animal found around the map.
  • Defeat 3 enemies with one attack, this can be done with an explosive barrel or pipe bomb.
  • Use the photo mode.
  • Set a Moroaicǎ on fire, they can be found in the dungeons of Castle Dimitrescu.
  • Push an enemy after guarding their attack.
  • Defeat an enemy from far away with a sniper rifle.
  • Shoot down a Soldat from the production line in the Factory.
  • Shoot down at least five flying crows.
  • At the beginning of the game, move the ball from the bedroom to the study.
  • Knock an arrow out of the sky with a close combat weapon.
  • Have 777, 7,777, or 77,777 lei. Starting at 0 lei, craft 111 rifle ammo (NOT sniper rifle ammo) and sell it to reach 777 lei. If you're good at math, have some fun and figure out other ways to reach 777 lei!

Complete Runs:

  • Complete the story on Casual difficulty or higher.
  • Complete the story on Standard difficulty or higher.
  • Complete the story on Hardcore difficulty or higher.
  • Complete the story on Village of Shadows difficulty. Since this is the hardest difficulty, it is recommended to play in NG+ with upgraded/infinite ammo weapons.

DLC Required:

  • Use Rose’s mutamycete powers 20 times.
  • Craft every item in Shadows of Rose.
  • Complete Shadows of Rose on Casual difficulty or higher.
  • Complete Shadows of Rose on Standard difficulty or higher.
  • Complete Shadows of Rose on Hardcore difficulty or higher.
  • Get a combo of 30 or more in The Mercenaries
  • Achieve an S rank or higher on all stages in The Mercenaries.
  • Complete the Bloody Village in The Mercenaries: Additional Orders
  • Complete the Bloody River in The Mercenaries: Additional Orders
