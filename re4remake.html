    • Resident Evil 4 Remake BioTracker

    Total Achievements: 46

    Story:

    • Defeat Del Lago in chapter 3.
    • Defeat Bitores Méndez in chapter 6.
    • Defeat Ramón Salazar in chapter 12.
    • Defeat Jack Krauser in chapter 14.
    • Defeat Osmund Saddler in chapter 16.

    Challenges:

    • Defeat Verdugo in chapter 10. Freeze him with the liquid nitrogen and use your strongest weapon. A rocket launcher can be used, but use it after exiting the first room you encounter him.
    • Complete the game in 8 hours or less.
    • In chapter 12, when in the clocktower's lift, do not get stopped. The lift stops when an enemy boards the lift. Kill enemies before they get on.
    • Defeat a garrador only using knives. In your first encounter with a garrador in chapter 7, stay crouched and attack the plagas in his back with your knife. If you do not have an infinite durability knife yet, bring many knives to the fight.
    • During Salazar's boss fight, when his mouth opens to attack, throw a grenade into his mouth.
    • Complete the game without using a recovery item.
    • Complete the game without interacting with the merchant.
    • Complete the game using knives and handguns. If the game forces you to use another weapon, it is excluded.

    Collectables:

    • Destroy 1 of 16 Clockwork Castellans. Refer to "Revolution Wind-Up" for more information.
    • Collect all treasures on the island treasure map in one playthrough.
    • Complete all 19 of the merchant's requests.
    • Collect all treasures on the village treasure map in one playthrough.
    • Destroy all 16 Clockwork Castellans. There is one every chapter.
    • Get an S rank in all levels of the shooting range minigame.
    • Collect all treasures on the castle treasure map in one playthrough.
    • Obtain all the weapons in the game.

    Miscellaneous:

    • Parry an enemy's attack with your knife. Check your settings to learn what your parry button is.
    • When speaking to the Merchant, upgrade a weapon once.
    • Complete 1 of 19 of the Merchant's requests.
    • When an enemy picks up Ashley, rescue her by damaging the enemy until it drops her.
    • Complete a game in the Merchant's shooting range minigame.
    • Use a cannon, found earliest in chapter 7, to defeat a zealot.
    • Get an exclusive upgrade for a weapon using an exclusive upgrade ticket.
    • Defeat 3 enemies at once with a flash grenade. Expose an enemy's Plagas to kill them with a flash grenade.
    • Kill 2 parasites inside a regenerador with one bullet. In chapter 13, after acquiring the biosensor scope, line up your rifle shot with two parasites inside of one of the regeneradors.
    • Sell a treasure for at least 100,000 ptas. This can be done when imbedding 5 different gems into the crown, which you can pick up in chapter 10 before the Verdugo fight.
    • In chapter 11, don't let your minecart take damage in both sections. You can save in between sections.
    • In chapter 16, escape with the water scooter without taking damage.
    • Destroy 5 targets with a single shot in the shooting range minigame. This can be easily done in level 4-C using the rifle.

    Complete Runs:

    • Complete the game on Standard or higher.
    • Complete the game on Hardcore or higher.
    • Complete the game on Professional.
    • Complete the game on Hardcore with an S+ rank.
    • Complete the game on Standard with an S+ rank.

    DLC Required:

    • Defeat El Gigante in chapter 3.
    • Defeat Pesanta in chapter 5.
    • Defeat Osmund Saddler in chapter 7.
    • Complete Separate Ways on Standard or higher.
    • Complete Separate Ways on Hardcore or higher.
    • Complete Separate Ways on Professional.
    • Complete Separate Ways on Professional with an S+ rank.
