    • Resident Evil 4 (2005) BioTracker

    Achievements:

    1. It Begins With a Ring

    In chapter 1-1, survive the village siege (Survive 3-4 minutes or defeat 15 enemies).

    1. A Rock and a Hard Place

    Defeat El Gigante in chapter 2-1.

    1. Secure the Ballistics

    Find Ashley in the church during chapter 2-2.

    1. A Bloodline Severed

    Defeat Bitores Mendez in chapter 2-3.

    1. The Castellan Falls

    Defeat Ramon Salazar in chapter 4-4.

    1. The Ties That Bind

    Defeat Jack Krauser in chapter 5-3.

    1. We're Going Home

    Defeat Osmund Saddler in chapter 5-4.

    1. A Terrifying Assassin

    In chapter 4-1, defeat Verdugo. The easiest way is to spawn Verdugo in, freeze him with a liquid nitrogen canister, and then use a rocket launcher.

    1. Do Not Shoot the Water!

    Do shoot the water, in chapter 1-3 before the Del Lago boss fight.

    1. The S Stands for Stylish!!

    Unlock all the costumes. To do this, complete the game once to unlock Special 1 set. Complete Separate Ways once to unlock Special 2 set.

    1. What Are They Worth?

    Acquire the bottle caps in game through the Merchant's Shooting Range minigame. They can be found in chapter 3-1, 4-1, 4-2, 5-1, and 5-3.

    1. A Heart of Steel

    Defeat the game in the Professional difficulty. Since there are no strict guidelines, save as much as possible. It is recommended to use the Special 2 costume set for Ashley, since the armor renders her immune to enemy attacks.
